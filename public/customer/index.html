01 — The Story

What is SbyNhamHub?

SbyNhamHub is a next-generation restaurant built for people who love dining out. We connect you to an unforgettable table — discovery, booking and rewards on a single platform that solves the pain of eating well.

Key insight: SbyNhamHub is fundamentally a hospitality business, not just a restaurant. Value comes from serving two groups at once — our guests and our chefs — so every visit compounds into the next.
02 — Why It Works

Problems we solve, deliciously

Dining out should be joy, not friction. We designed every touchpoint to remove the frustration you didn't even know you had.

For Diners

  • Calling restaurants only to find no availability — frustrating and slow.
  • No central place to compare cuisine, price, location, and reviews.
  • No transparency on real-time table availability.
  • Risk of wasted journeys — arriving to find long waits or no tables.
  • No incentives or rewards for loyal dining habits.
  • Trusted, verified reviews are hard to find.

For Our Kitchen

  • Staff tied up answering reservation phone calls.
  • Empty tables off-peak with no smart fill strategy.
  • No guest history to personalise your next visit.
  • High no-show rates with no prevention mechanism.
  • Limited digital marketing reach and online visibility.
  • No yield management — unlike hotels or airlines.
Market context: 83% of diners use smartphones to look up restaurants before visiting — yet the industry remains overwhelmingly phone-driven. SbyNhamHub captures this digital migration moment.
